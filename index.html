    GERMAN VERB GUIDE

    German conjugation from A1 to C2

    Build your German verb skills step by step. Each CEFR level adds more vocabulary, tense control and complex verb patterns.

    A1

    Master high-frequency verbs in the present tense and essential forms such as sein, haben and werden.

    Example: ich bin · du hast · er geht
    A2

    Practise common irregular verbs, Perfekt and verbs used in everyday conversations.

    Example: ich kannte · du hast geholfen
    B1

    Work with separable verbs, narrative past forms and a wider range of irregular patterns.

    Example: ich rufe an · wir sind angekommen
    B2

    Strengthen formal and abstract verbs while controlling compound tenses and subordinate-clause vocabulary.

    Example: sie übernimmt · wir haben beigetragen
    C1

    Practise precise academic and professional verbs, including complex prefixes and advanced mood forms.

    Example: er gewährleistet · sie widerspräche
    C2

    Refine rare, idiomatic and highly nuanced verbs used in sophisticated written and spoken German.

    Example: es obliegt · sie bekräftigten
